Exercise 1: The four practice rules

What you will learn: You will learn that some clicks on the live system can never be taken back. You will also learn the four rules that keep today's practice safe.

What is happening: Your facilitator welcomes you and talks you through a one-page brief. On your desk you have the brief, a printed cheat sheet open at Stage 1, this workbook and a pen.

Your steps:

  1. Read the title of the brief.
    • Brief title: Run a workshop from first draft to certificate today
  2. Write the question at the top of this workbook.
    • Write: Is this mine to do, or do I recognise it and hand it to Finance?
  3. Read your role. Your account has the "Workshop creator" tick. It does NOT have the "Finance" tick. So some buttons will simply not be on your screen. That is on purpose. It is not a fault.
  4. Read what each tick lets a person do. A tick is a permission that a super admin switches on for a staff account.
    • "Workshop creator" tick (yours): create, edit, publish and close workshops. Upload and remove files. Open check-in early. It is why you see the "Create Workshop" tab. You can also delete a workshop for good with "Delete workshop", but only while nobody has paid. It does not let you change the reminder settings.
    • "Finance" tick (not yours): the money jobs. Mark a booking paid. Create, edit or switch off coupons. Cancel or remove a booking. Send a payment reminder by hand. Correct a payer's email. Pull the export that has money in it.
    • In the Western Cape today: nobody has the "Finance" tick and there is no auditor. So Finance here means the super admins.
  5. Read what you will have done by 13:10. You will have created, published and shared a TRAINING workshop, followed a booking, handed a payment to Finance, run check-in, marked attendance, sent a certificate and pulled an export.
  6. Read the order of the day. You follow one workshop's life in this order:
    1. Create it yourself, from your own printed planning sheet.
    2. Publish.
    3. Share.
    4. Follow bookings.
    5. Chase payment, and hand the money to Finance.
    6. Handle changes.
    7. Run the day.
    8. Attendance.
    9. Certificates.
    10. Exports.
  7. Learn the four practice rules.
    • Rule 1, which workshop: only touch workshops titled "TRAINING - <topic> - delete after". Nothing else.
    • Rule 2, which email: every booking you make uses your own email. Never a real member's email.
      • Your bookings: <Trainee work email> or <Trainee personal email>
      • Your facilitator's bookings: loxly@lbtec.co.za
      • When an exercise asks for a wrong address on purpose: wrong.address@example.com
    • Rule 3, how they pay: EFT only. EFT is a plain bank transfer into NAPTOSA's account. The bank details on the EFT instructions are for training only. Never pay them.
    • Rule 4, cards: never type a real card number anywhere. Not even on the "Card (demonstration)" option.
  8. Read why the rules matter. These are the exact warnings you will meet later today:
    • On the "Mark EFT paid" box: "A receipt is emailed immediately and this cannot be undone."
    • On certificates: "This cannot be undone."
    • Every email the system sends is a real email.
  9. Turn the brief over and say the four rules back to your facilitator in your own words.

Why your own email matters: the system emails the address on the booking. Because you use your own address, you receive and read every email your bookings send. You see exactly what a member sees, and no real member gets anything.

If you book the same workshop twice with the same email: you see "Possible existing registration" with the reason "same email, same telephone". Click "Save as additional entry".

How role-plays work today: sometimes your facilitator plays a caller, such as a member, a school asking about a booking or payment, or a colleague. When he says "Narrator", he is telling you what happens on screen. When he says "Coach", he is himself again. Hint slips lie face down beside you. Turn one over whenever you want.

What you should see:

When to hand it to Finance

Hand it over whenever the job is one of these:

  • Marking a booking paid ("Mark EFT paid").
  • Creating, editing or switching off a coupon.
  • Cancelling a booking, or removing a cancelled one.
  • Sending one person a payment reminder by hand ("Remind").
  • Correcting a payer's email ("Correct payer email").
  • Pulling the export with money in it.

A missing button is your cue. Reloading or "Refresh" will not make it appear.

When you hand it over, pass on three things: the booking name, the workshop, and what is needed.

What you say: "This is a Finance job. [Finance person], please [what is needed] for [booking name] on [workshop]. [Any fact you checked, such as the payment date]."

What the system cannot do yet, and what to do instead

  • Registration closes by itself overnight, not the moment a workshop ends. From the night of 8 to 9 October 2026, a nightly run at 00:05 closes every published workshop whose end time was before midnight. Once a workshop's end time passes, the website Events page drops it on its own. But until the 00:05 run that night, the booking link and its QR code keep taking bookings. After the run the workshop sits in "Closed archive". It refuses new bookings, an unpaid booking on it can no longer get EFT details or pay by card, and its payment reminders stop.
    • What to do instead: to stop bookings sooner, open "Published Links", click the group "Needs closure", select the ended workshop and click "Close registrations". The workshop moves to "Closed archive" and the link stops taking bookings at once.
    • If the event moved: edit the dates. Do not press "Reopen registrations" on a workshop whose dates have passed, because the next 00:05 run closes it again.
    • The line you will see on an ended, open workshop: "This link is still open, but the workshop dates have passed. Edit the dates or close registrations before sharing it."
  • "Delete workshop" only works while nobody has paid. Once a booking on your TRAINING workshop is marked paid, it cannot be deleted. The box then says: "A payment has been recorded for this workshop, so it cannot be deleted. Payment records are always kept. Cancel the workshop instead. To stop new bookings now, use Close registrations."
    • What to do instead: "delete after" in the title means you click "Close registrations" when the day ends.
  • A paid mark, a sent email or an issued certificate cannot be undone. Receipts cannot be deleted either.
    • What to do instead: prevent it. Use the TRAINING workshop, your own email on every booking, EFT only and never a real card. If something real goes wrong, stop and tell your facilitator.
  • You cannot do money jobs. The buttons are not on your screen, and the system refuses them even if you reach them another way.
    • What to do instead: find the booking, check the facts and hand it to a named Finance person. You can still pull "Export Excel" without money.
  • You see the Western Cape only. You cannot help with another province's workshop.
    • What to do instead: send the colleague to the super admin, or to their own province's staff.
  • Reminder settings are one list for every province. Changing them for one booking changes them for everyone.
    • What to do instead: look only and save nothing. If it really needs changing, raise it with the super admin.
  • Bank details belong to the super admin. Every save on "Payment details" emails a change alert to real staff, and "Confirm these details" changes the bank account real payers see.
    • What to do instead: look only, never save, and pass the question to the super admin.
  • "Refresh" reloads workshops only. A coupon change will not show after it.
    • What to do instead: click away and back, or reload the browser.

Set up and publish a workshop

In this part you plan a workshop from your own planning sheet. You set it up, make it public and share the link. Everything else you do today hangs off this workshop: the bookings, the payments, the day itself and the certificates.

Words you will see

  • Workshop creator tick: a setting on your staff login. It lets you create, edit and publish workshops. You have it today.
  • Finance tick: a setting that turns on the money actions, like marking a payment as received or making a discount code. You do not have it. In the Western Cape nobody has it, and there is no auditor. Finance here means the super admins.
  • Toast: the small message that pops up after you click a button. It tells you what just happened.
  • Pill: the small coloured label on a workshop card. Gold "Draft" means private. Green "Published" means live.

Before you start: four rules to remember

You will learn that a new workshop is always private until you publish it.

  1. Every new workshop starts as a draft. A draft is private. Nobody outside the office can see it or book it.
  2. The order is always the same. Draft first. Then check the preview. Then publish.
  3. Do not press Enter in a one-line box, like the title or a price. Enter saves the whole form. Move from box to box with Tab or the mouse. Only the Description, the Venue and address, and the Terms boxes take Enter as a new line.
  4. The public link is built from the title on the day you create the workshop. It never changes, even if you edit the title later. So get the title right the first time.

What the system cannot do yet, and what to do instead

  • Registration does not close the moment a workshop ends. The website's Events page drops a workshop as soon as its end time passes, and a nightly run at 00:05 closes its registrations. Until that run, the booking link and its QR code still take bookings. Instead: to stop bookings sooner, open "Published Links" > "Needs closure" and click "Close registrations" on each workshop that is done. If an event moved, edit its dates. "Reopen registrations" on a workshop whose dates have passed is undone at the next 00:05 run.
  • No refunds. Instead: a refund is a bank transfer Finance makes outside the app, with the Provincial CEO's approval. Pass it to Finance.
  • No way to cancel a whole workshop. Instead: click "Close registrations" and contact the people who booked yourself.
  • "Delete workshop" only works while nobody has paid. Instead: once anyone has paid, close registrations. Deleting cannot be undone. Closing keeps the record and can be reopened.
  • You cannot mark an EFT paid, send a payment reminder or cancel a booking. Instead: say the hand-off sentence to Finance.
  • No staff screen can fix a person's name. A certificate freezes the name, points, SACE number and dates when it is issued. Instead: before the certificate goes out, stop and ask the super admin to fix the name. Remember the automatic run sends it at 08:00 the morning after the workshop.
  • No automatic "details changed" email. If you move a workshop, nobody is told. Instead: change the dates in "Edit details", then contact the paid people yourself. Their emails are in your export.
  • The "Workshop starting soon" email is switched off. In the Western Cape only a super admin can switch it on. Even when it is on, it never reaches someone who booked after the reminder moment. Instead: on the day, use "Email check-in details to attendees".
  • Marking someone present or absent sends no email while the attendance emails are switched off.
  • A sent email cannot be called back. That covers EFT instructions, receipts, check-in emails and certificates.
